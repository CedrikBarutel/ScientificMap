# Curated Vienna Biophysics / Soft-Matter Source Notes

Curated on 2026-07-09 from official public institutional pages. The project stores source URLs per person in `data/processed/people.csv` and the raw curated table in `data/raw/vienna_biophysics_people.csv`.

## Scope

The added records focus on Vienna-area researchers relevant to biophysics, active/living matter, soft matter, microscopy, tissue biomechanics, and quantitative biological networks.

## Manual-contact policy used here

Some official university pages obfuscate addresses with `[at]` or only show a “send email” button. For those records, the `email` field was left blank and the profile URL was stored instead. The campaign exporter now validates email syntax and will skip malformed/obfuscated contacts.
